Unitly — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

1. Overview

Unitly ("the App") is a unit-price comparison utility developed for personal use. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

Unitly collects no personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

All data created within Unitly — including comparison history, savings records, shopping lists, and app settings — is stored locally on your device using your device's secure local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No payment information
  • No device identifiers

5. Third-Party Services

Unitly does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

6. Children's Privacy

Unitly does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected at all, the App is safe for users of all ages.

7. Data Deletion

All data stored by Unitly can be deleted at any time directly within the App via Settings → Clear All Data, or by uninstalling the App from your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in a future version of the App, the updated policy will be published in this repository. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For questions or concerns about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer via Gmail, df.dev21@gmail.com.
